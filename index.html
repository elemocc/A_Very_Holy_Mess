🧩 Twine

Interactive Narrative Prototype

The Twine prototype is used to test the interactive narrative structure of the game. It follows the player through the main quest, from the first encounter with Saint George to the final battle against the dragon.

Through Twine, we prototyped dialogues, choices, challenges, transitions between artworks, and narrative consequences.

Figma prototype coming soon

Companion App

The companion app acts as the digital game master. It guides the player, activates the artworks, gives clues, validates answers, manages the timer, and connects each challenge to the story.

Cards

Artwork Cards and Challenge Cards allow the experience to be played at home. The cards reproduce selected paintings and act as physical keys to unlock digital content through the companion app.

App

The app supports both museum and at-home play. In the museum, it works as an AR tool; at home, it activates digital content through cards and QR codes.

Academic Disclaimer and AI Statement

Academic Disclaimer

This project was developed as part of a university exam for the course Digital Heritage and Multimedia, within the Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge programme at the Alma Mater Studiorum – Università di Bologna.

A Very Holy Mess is an academic design prototype created for educational and research purposes. It is not an official product, application, or commissioned project of the Pinacoteca Nazionale di Bologna.

Most of the photographs used in this repository were taken by the authors inside the museum with their own camera equipment and are included only as part of the academic prototype and design documentation.

All references to the museum, its artworks, and its collection are used exclusively for study, design experimentation, and cultural heritage prototyping. Any external artwork images or additional visual materials, if included, belong to their respective institutions or rights holders.

AI Statement

The authors used AI-assisted tools to support the writing process and the generation of images and videos as samples of what the final product could look like.

The AI tools used are ChatGPT by OpenAI and Gemini by Google for writing support and image processing, and Pippit by ByteDance for video generation.

All content, design decisions, research findings, game mechanics, narrative structure, and project outcomes are the result of the authors’ own work. AI tools were used only as writing, visual, and graphic support tools, and not as sources of data, research evidence, or design decisions.
